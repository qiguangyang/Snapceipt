Snapceipt is an Australian (AUD / 10% GST) receipt + expense tracker. Receipt scanning, manual entry, mileage, work-from-home logging, reports, and sharing receipts from other apps are all free. Quotes, Invoices and Email-in are Pro features, unlocked by an auto-renewing Apple In-App Purchase subscription.

SIGNING IN (no demo account needed):
- Easiest: on the landing screen tap "Sign in with Apple" and authenticate with your own Apple ID. This signs you in immediately — no verification code required.
- Or tap "Sign in with Email" to register with your own email address; the app sends a 6-digit verification code to that address to complete sign-up.

NEW IN THIS VERSION (1.1.0): Share receipts straight from other apps — from Mail, Files, Photos or any share sheet, choose Snapceipt to import a receipt or invoice. This is a free feature, testable on any account.

REVIEWING PRO FEATURES: To review Quotes, Invoices and Email-in, start the subscription — it is an auto-renewing Apple In-App Purchase and is free to purchase in the App Review sandbox.

PERMISSIONS / DATA:
- Camera + Photos: to capture/import receipt images.
- Receipt OCR sends only the receipt image/text to our extraction processor (Google Gemini); no account name or email is shared.
- Subscriptions are auto-renewing Apple In-App Purchases billed in AUD.
- Account deletion is available in-app: Profile tab -> Account -> Delete account (irreversibly deletes all data server-side).
