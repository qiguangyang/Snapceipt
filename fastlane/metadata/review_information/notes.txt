Snapceipt is an Australian (AUD / 10% GST) receipt + expense tracker. Receipt scanning, manual entry, mileage, work-from-home logging and reports are free. Quotes, Invoices and Email-in are Pro features, unlocked by an auto-renewing Apple In-App Purchase subscription.

SIGNING IN (demo account in demo_user.txt / demo_password.txt):
1. On the landing screen tap "Sign in" then "Continue with email".
2. Enter the demo email + password above.
3. On a first sign-in from a new device the app emails a 6-digit verification code (new-device two-factor). For THIS demo account, enter the verification code 123456 to complete sign-in (you do not need the emailed code).
The demo account is Pro-entitled, so Quotes, Invoices and Email-in are all reviewable. Sign in with Apple is also supported.

PERMISSIONS / DATA:
- Camera + Photos: to capture/import receipt images.
- Receipt OCR sends only the receipt image/text to our extraction processor (Google Gemini); no account name or email is shared.
- Subscriptions are auto-renewing Apple In-App Purchases billed in AUD.
- Account deletion is available in-app: Profile tab -> Account -> Delete account (irreversibly deletes all data server-side).
