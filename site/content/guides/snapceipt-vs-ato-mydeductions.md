If you're a sole trader hunting for an ATO myDeductions alternative, it's worth understanding what the ATO's own free tool actually does before you pay for anything else. myDeductions is genuinely handy — and for plenty of people it's all they'll ever need. For others, it runs out of road the moment GST or invoicing enters the picture. This guide compares the free ATO myDeductions tool with Snapceipt honestly, so you can pick the right fit for how you actually work.

## The short answer

Use **ATO myDeductions** if your tax affairs are simple: a modest list of deductions, no GST registration, and you don't mind typing each expense in. It's free, it comes straight from the ATO, and it feeds neatly into your tax return.

Reach for **Snapceipt** (or a similar dedicated app) if you're registered for GST, lodge a quarterly BAS, send quotes and invoices, or simply want the receipt read for you instead of typing every line. You'll get more automation, but the advanced pieces sit behind a paid Pro plan.

## What myDeductions does well

myDeductions lives inside the free ATO app, and for a government tool it's surprisingly capable. It lets you:

- Record work-related and sole trader expenses, plus business income
- Photograph a receipt and attach the image as evidence
- Log car trips for the cents-per-kilometre or logbook method
- Upload your records at year end to **prefill your tax return** in myTax, or email them to your registered tax agent

That prefill step is the real winner. Because it's the ATO's own tool, the data flows straight into your return with no exporting or reformatting. If you claim car expenses, the trip logger is a tidy way to build a logbook — note that the cents-per-kilometre rate is 88 cents per km for the 2024–25 year, but the ATO reviews it annually, so confirm the current figure at ato.gov.au.

And it's free, forever. There's no subscription and no upsell.

## Where myDeductions stops

The catch is that myDeductions is designed to prefill one annual income tax return — nothing more. That means:

- **Manual entry.** You photograph the receipt, but you still type the supplier, amount and category yourself. The image is stored, not read.
- **No GST or BAS support.** It doesn't split out the GST on each purchase or produce a BAS summary. If you're registered for GST, it can't help you complete your quarterly statement.
- **No invoicing or quotes.** It's a record-keeping tool, not a way to bill clients or send a professional quote.
- **Built around device storage.** Your records sit in the app until you upload them, so it pays to stay on top of backups.

None of that is a flaw — it's just the boundary of what a free tax-return helper is meant to do. For a fuller setup that goes beyond it, see our guide to [expense tracking for sole traders](/guides/expense-tracking-sole-traders).

## Where Snapceipt fits

Snapceipt starts where myDeductions stops. You snap a receipt and its AI reads the merchant, total, GST and category, so the record is built without typing. Because it captures the GST on every purchase, it keeps [BAS-ready records](/guides/bas-due-dates-and-how-to-lodge) — a genuine time-saver if you lodge quarterly. It also handles [quotes and invoices](/guides/quote-invoice-app-australia), which myDeductions doesn't touch at all.

To be fair about the trade-off: [Snapceipt](/) can't prefill your tax return the way the ATO's own tool can, and the advanced GST, BAS and invoicing features are part of the paid Pro plan. Snapping and storing receipts is free; the automation you pay for is the retyping you avoid.

## Which should you use?

Be honest about your situation. If you're a side-hustler or new sole trader with a short list of deductions and no GST registration, **free myDeductions is the sensible choice** — it costs nothing and prefills your return. There's no reason to pay for features you won't use.

Once you register for GST, start lodging a BAS, or begin invoicing clients, the manual, GST-blind nature of myDeductions starts to cost you time and accuracy. That's the point where a dedicated app like Snapceipt earns its keep. Plenty of sole traders begin with myDeductions and graduate as the business grows — and there's nothing wrong with running both while you decide.

This is general information, not tax advice — check ato.gov.au or a registered tax agent for your situation.
