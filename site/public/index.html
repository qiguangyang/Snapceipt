FAQ

Good to know.

Which receipts can Snapceipt read?

Paper or digital — snap it with your camera, import from Photos or Files, or forward an emailed receipt to your private Snapceipt inbox. It reads the merchant, date, total, GST and category automatically.

Do I need to be GST-registered?

No. Snapceipt works for any Australian sole trader or household. If you are GST-registered, Pro adds BAS-ready summaries and a quarterly export.

Is my data private?

Yes. No ads, no trackers, and we never sell your data. Receipts are processed only to extract their details, and you can delete your account and data any time from inside the app.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Capture receipts with no signal and they'll sync automatically once you're back online — your receipts are never stuck on a single device.
