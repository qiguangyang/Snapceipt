Snapceipt makes money from Pro subscriptions — not from your data. There are no ads and no trackers, and we never sell your information. Your subscription funds an independent, Australian-made app.

Pricing questions

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes — capture and auto-sort receipts, extract GST and sync across devices at no cost, for as long as you like. No credit card required.

Is there a free trial of Pro?

Yes. Pro starts with a 14-day free trial. You won't be charged if you cancel before it ends.

Can I cancel any time?

Absolutely. Manage or cancel your subscription any time in your Apple ID settings — Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

Monthly or annual?

Both. Annual ($79/yr) works out about a third cheaper than paying monthly ($9.99/mo). You can switch between them from the App Store.

How am I billed?

Payment is handled securely by Apple through your App Store account. All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Refunds are handled by Apple under the App Store terms.
