Privacy Policy

Effective 15 June 2026 · Last updated 15 June 2026 · Snapceipt ("we", "us", "our")

Snapceipt is an Australian-made receipt and expense app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the control you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Privacy at a glance
🔒
No ads, no trackers.
We don't use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
🚫
We never sell your data.
Your information is not shared for marketing — ever.
📷
Only receipt text is sent for reading.
Never your name, email or account details.
🗑️
Delete everything any time.
Account → Delete Account removes your data immediately.

What we collect

Type
What it includes
Account details
Your email address and, if you use Sign in with Apple, the name and email Apple shares with us.
Receipts & transactions
Receipt photos you capture and the details extracted from them — merchant, amounts, GST, category, notes.
Your app content
Things you enter: clients and quotes, vehicles and mileage, work-from-home logs, loyalty cards, budgets and tax settings.
Device & push token
If you enable notifications: a push token plus basic device info (model, OS version, timezone) to deliver the alerts you asked for.
Diagnostics
Limited server logs (e.g. error and request records) to keep the service running and secure. No third-party analytics.

We don't ask for your location, contacts, or any data we don't need to run the app.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core service — capturing, reading, categorising, storing and syncing your receipts and records.
  • To send notifications you've turned on, such as budget alerts and BAS reminders.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we don't use the content of your receipts to train AI models.

Who processes your data

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the app. They process data only on our instructions, and we require them to protect it to a standard consistent with this policy and the APPs.

  • Cloudflare — hosting and storage. Receipt images are kept in object storage; your records in our database.
  • DeepSeek — to read a receipt, its extracted text is sent to DeepSeek's AI to identify the merchant, total, GST and category. Only receipt text is shared — never your name, email or account details.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI — if you forward a receipt to your private Snapceipt inbox, its image is read (OCR) here before the same text-only extraction.
  • Apple — processes your sign-in if you use Sign in with Apple, and handles all subscription payments through the App Store.
Some of these providers may process data on servers outside Australia. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information is handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Sharing & disclosure

We don't sell your personal information and we don't share it for anyone's marketing. We disclose information only to the processors above to run the app, or where we're required to by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your account, receipts, images and transactions are removed immediately; residual copies in encrypted infrastructure backups are purged within 30 days.

Your choices & rights

  • Access & correction — view and edit your data in the app, or email us for a copy.
  • Delete your account — in the app: Account → Delete Account. This is immediate and irreversible.
  • Notifications — turn push notifications on or off any time in Settings.
  • Complaints — contact us first; you may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Data security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted, and we use reputable infrastructure (Cloudflare) to protect it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take protecting your information seriously.

Children

Snapceipt is intended for adults managing their own finances and isn't directed at children under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy, or want a copy of your data? Email support@snapceipt.cc and we'll respond within a couple of business days.
