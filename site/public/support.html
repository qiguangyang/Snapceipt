Support

Snapceipt is in private beta. If something looks wrong — a receipt that won't extract, a sync that didn't happen, a sign-in loop — we want to hear about it.

Get help

Email support@snapceipt.cc and include what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help.

Sign-in issues

If a magic-link email doesn't arrive within a couple of minutes, check spam, or use Sign in with Apple instead.

Delete your account

In the app: Account → Delete Account. It removes all your data immediately and permanently.
