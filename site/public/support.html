Frequently asked

My magic-link email hasn't arrived

Give it a couple of minutes and check your spam or junk folder. Still nothing? Use Sign in with Apple instead, or email us and we'll help. Make sure you entered the same email you signed up with.

A receipt didn't extract correctly

Tap the receipt and edit any field by hand — merchant, total, GST or category. Snapceipt learns from your corrections. For best results, capture the whole receipt in good light on a flat surface.

A receipt didn't sync

Check you're online and signed in. Captures made offline sync automatically once you're back on a connection. If something seems stuck, pull to refresh, or sign out and back in.

How do I export for my accountant?

On Pro, open Reports and choose export, or use the BAS card for a BAS-ready summary. You can email a clean PDF and CSV straight to your accountant.

How do I manage or cancel my Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Snapceipt. There you can switch between monthly and annual, or cancel. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Snapceipt and choose Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Account → Delete Account. Your account, receipts, images and transactions are removed immediately; residual backup copies are purged within 30 days. This can't be undone.

How is my data handled?

No ads, no trackers, and we never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.

System requirements

Snapceipt runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Capture works on-device; sync and receipt reading need an internet connection.
